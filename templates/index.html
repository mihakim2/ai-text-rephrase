Rephraser

Turn AI-written text into something a PhD student would actually write.

How it works

This app rewrites AI-written prose to read like an actual human (specifically: a PhD student) wrote it. Two modes:

  • Deep mode (recommended) — paragraph-level rewriting with structure preservation. Headers, lists, code, and math are never touched. Each prose paragraph is first scored by a blind AI detector; if it already sounds human enough, it passes through. Otherwise it's rewritten by Claude Sonnet using paragraph context and either your own writing samples or an inferred voice profile. A prompted critic and the blind detector then independently judge the rewrite; both must clear thresholds, plus a similarity check ensures the rewrite isn't a near-copy.
  • Quick mode — sentence-by-sentence rewriting. Faster, simpler, less stylistically cohesive.

Why these choices

  • Cross-family judging — writer is Claude, critic and detector are Gemini. Different training data, different blind spots.
  • Blind detector is the honesty signal — the prompted critic Goodharts against its own rubric; the blind detector has no rubric and is harder to game.
  • Similarity gate — the critic sees both the original and the rewrite. A near-copy is rejected even if it sounds human.
  • User samples beat inferred voice — paste a few real paragraphs of your own writing in Deep mode and the rewriter matches that voice directly.
  • Triage skips good writing — paragraphs that already score human enough are preserved verbatim.

Models used

  • Voice calibration: Claude Opus 4.7
  • Paragraph/sentence rewriting: Claude Sonnet 4.6
  • Intent extraction: Claude Haiku 4.5
  • Critic + blind detector: Gemini 2.5 Pro
